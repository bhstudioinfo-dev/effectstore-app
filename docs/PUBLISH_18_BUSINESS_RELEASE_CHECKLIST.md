## Data categories requiring decisions

Accounts (email, phone, password hash), device identifiers, payments/proof images, gift/usernames/avatar URLs, logs, custom-effect metadata, purchased entitlements and support diagnostics need documented purpose, retention, access, deletion and incident handling.

## Release gate

Obtain professional review for consumer subscription/refund terms, privacy/data protection, content licensing/copyright, platform/trademark use and business/payment disclosures before accepting paid public customers. Do not copy generic legal templates without counsel and actual operational decisions.
